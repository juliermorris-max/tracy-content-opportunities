How this shortlist is selected

Matches use job descriptions as well as titles: UX writing, content design, strategy, governance, editorial workflows, SEO and localization. Contract and part-time work receive a preference, alongside seniority and health or wellbeing experience. Match scores are rule-based rankings, not probabilities or employer endorsements.

Remote Europe, EU, EMEA and worldwide listings reasonably suggest Spain eligibility; these are labelled for confirmation. US-only remote roles and office roles outside Barcelona are excluded. Listings with unclear seniority or additional language requirements are flagged. Annual compensation around €70,000, or contract rates around USD 500/day or USD 60/hour, are preferences for your review; currencies are not converted and missing pay is not guessed.

Every displayed posting passed a page and application-link check during the search. Availability can change later. Sources publish different pay conventions; check the full posting for rates, hours, benefits, travel and requirements. This report is a curated selection, not an exhaustive search.

Searches are intended for approximately 08:17 Barcelona time, with daylight-saving adjustment. GitHub may delay scheduled runs. A failed search preserves the last successful report.
